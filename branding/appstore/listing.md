## Description

Keep receipts where you can find them.

RS Receipts groups receipts into reports — a trip, a project, a month — and totals each one as
you go. Photograph a receipt, crop it straight, and it's filed. Or skip the photo and type the
amount.

Everything stays on your device. There's no account, no sign-up and no subscription, and
nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it.

**Reports that add themselves up**
Every receipt carries a description, an amount and a date. The report keeps the running total
and the date range. Drag reports into whatever order suits you.

**Receipts, photographed or typed**
Take a photo or pick one from your library, then drag the corners to crop. Not every receipt
needs a picture — type an amount and move on.

**Export the way your accounts team wants it**
A PDF with a summary table and one page per receipt image, or a ZIP holding a CSV and the
images numbered to match. Both go wherever the file picker reaches: Google Drive, iCloud
Drive, a folder on your device.

**Back up to a single file**
One file holds every report, receipt and photo. Save it where you like and restore it on any
device — including an Android phone.

**Share a report with someone**
Put a report in a shared file and work on it together. Both people can add, edit and delete at
the same time; changes merge by when they were made, and photos travel inside the file. Each
sync tells you exactly what it did.

**Works with Android**
Backups and shared reports move between iPhone, iPad and Android in both directions. One
person on iOS and the other on Android read and write the same file.

**Worth knowing**
One currency for the whole app, taken from your region. Deleting can't be undone. A shared
file is rewritten in full on every sync, photos included.
